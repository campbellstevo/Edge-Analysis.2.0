# Edge Analysis — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 24 September 2026 · Operated from Victoria, Australia · Contact: campbellstevo@gmail.com_

## The short version
Your trading journal stays in **your** Notion workspace. Edge Analysis reads it to draw your dashboard. We keep the minimum needed to run the service and nothing else.

## What we store
- **Account link:** which journal database you connected (and its template), keyed to your Notion account id, plus a daily count of chat questions for the usage limit. We do **not** keep your name or email.
- **A cache** of your journal on the server, so pages load fast. It is refreshed as you use the app and deleted once it is a day old; it is not an archive.
- **On your own device** (browser storage): your Notion sign-in, theme, filter and plan preferences. Clearing your browser storage removes them.
- **Feedback you send** through the in-app feedback box, delivered to a private Notion page.
- **Error reports:** if a page fails to draw, a report of what broke and where (not your journal) goes to our crash reporter, Sentry.

## What we never do
We don't sell or share your data. We don't run ads. We don't use your journal to train anything. We can't place trades — the app only reads your journal.

## The analyst chat
The chat answers from your own numbers on our server; your questions and journal are not sent anywhere else.

## Deleting your data
In the app: ⋯ menu → Journals → **Disconnect**. That deletes your account link and the cached copy of your journal from our server, and the sign-in saved in that browser. Your journal is yours in Notion regardless; deleting our copy doesn't touch it.
