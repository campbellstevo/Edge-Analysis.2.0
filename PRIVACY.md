# Edge Analysis — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 31 July 2026 · Operated from Victoria, Australia · Contact: campbellstevo@gmail.com_

## The short version
Your trading journal stays in **your** Notion workspace. Edge Analysis reads it to draw your dashboard. We keep the minimum needed to run the service and nothing else.

## What we store
- **Account link:** your Notion name, email address, and which journal template/database you connected. Needed so the app remembers you between visits.
- **A short-lived cache** of your journal on the server, so pages load fast. It is refreshed on a cycle and overwritten; it is not an archive.
- **On your own device** (browser storage): sign-in, theme, filter and plan preferences. Clearing your browser storage removes them.
- **If you connect WHOOP:** an access token stored in a private page inside your own Notion workspace — not on our server.
- **Feedback you send** through the in-app feedback box, delivered to a private Notion page.

## What we never do
We don't sell or share your data. We don't run ads. We don't use your journal to train anything. We can't place trades — the app only reads your journal.

## Optional AI chat
The built-in analyst answers from your own numbers on the server. If the AI upgrade is enabled, your question plus a compact statistical summary (never your raw journal) is sent to Anthropic's API to compose the answer.

## Deleting your data
Disconnect in the app, or email us — we'll delete your account link and cache. Your journal is yours in Notion regardless; deleting our copy of the link doesn't touch it.
